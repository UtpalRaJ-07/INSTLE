# INSTLE REAL ESTATE — LAUNCH COSTS AND PAYMENT PLAN

Prepared 29 September 2026. All rupee ranges below are **planning allowances proposed for this project**, not vendor quotes, current market-price claims, or guarantees. They exclude applicable taxes, foreign-exchange/card costs, usage overages, and internal staff time. Obtain actual quotations before purchase. No purchase, subscription, campaign, or deployment has been made by this planning work.

## What to pay for first

1. **A domain in the owner's account**, after choosing and approving the exact name. The domain remains TO BE CONFIRMED until it is purchased and explicitly supplied. Compare renewal as well as first-year price.
2. **Commercially permitted hosting** for the existing Next.js application when ready to deploy. Confirm runtime support, region/data needs, commercial-use terms, previews, recovery, and billing limits.
3. **An approved lead destination** before turning on real-data forms. If this is not ready, consider an approved call-only initial release with forms disabled; do not use temporary memory or an unreviewed spreadsheet for real customers.
4. **A business mailbox**, if required for the selected operations/notice/profile setup. Choose the address after the domain is confirmed; do not invent a business email now.

Local development and preparation can proceed before paying for a domain or production hosting. Existing Claude/tooling costs should be checked before buying another subscription. No public AI chatbot or OpenAI API spend is required merely to make the website discoverable in ChatGPT search.

### Verified provider facts versus estimates

The official [Vercel pricing page](https://vercel.com/pricing) displayed Pro at **US$20/month** when checked, with usage/seat/add-on details that must be confirmed at checkout. The same page says Hobby is for personal, non-commercial use. Vercel is an option, not an approved vendor; do not budget on its Hobby plan for this brokerage.

[Cloudflare Registrar](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/registrar/) describes at-cost registration and renewal pricing. That does not establish availability or a rupee price for an unchosen domain. Check supported TLDs, exact registration/renewal costs, nameserver requirements, and control before choosing it.

[Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/us/business-profile/) provides a free business profile. [Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about) provides Google's site search-monitoring tools. There is no payment that purchases an organic local ranking; see [Google's local-ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091).

## What should not receive money

- Fake review packages, fake customer accounts, or purchased testimonials.
- Guaranteed Google/Maps/ChatGPT ranking packages or guaranteed AI mentions.
- Spam backlinks, private blog networks, or mass directory submissions with false offices.
- Unneeded WordPress SEO plugins for this Next.js project.
- A duplicate website builder subscription when the chosen hosting already runs the application.
- A paid SSL certificate by default if the selected host provides an appropriate certificate; confirm the actual plan.
- Multiple local business profiles for nonexistent branches.
- Ads before the team can receive and handle enquiries and track actual outcomes.

## Purchase and account checklist

For each selected service, record the owner account, recovery contact, price/date/currency, tax treatment,
renewal date, commercial terms, spending limits, cancellation/export procedure, and who administers it.
For personal-data processors, also record approved destination, access, processing terms, retention,
security review, backup implications, and deployment region as the project requires.
Keep business ownership and access independent of a freelancer's personal account.
Never paste secrets or customer records into the planning prompt.

## Practical spend sequence

**Now:** review the handoff, confirm real service capability and identity, build locally, choose actual operating processes.

**When the build is reviewable:** obtain domain/hosting/destination quotes; approve the implementation and notices; prepare account access and releases.

**At authorized launch:** purchase/set the confirmed domain and chosen infrastructure; connect the approved destination or use the approved call-only mode; verify production indexing and phone access.

**After launch:** measure baseline outcomes, collect genuine feedback, and expand researched coverage. Approve recurring content or ads based on actual capacity and results, not promised rankings.
